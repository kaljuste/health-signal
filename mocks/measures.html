What we measure,
and why these

A value earns its place only if the assistant can explain it and the crew can act on it. Each of the 76 values below passed four tests.

  • It can be measuredA sensor that flies today, a short self-test, or an estimate that is labelled as one.
  • It has a sourced limitA NASA standard, a clinical guideline or a published study. Our own assumptions are marked INF.
  • Its course is knownWhat it does in weightlessness, and what is expected at Mars gravity, from flight data or the best analog.
  • Something followsA warning, an action for the crew, or an instruction from Earth.
