Log of this walk

    Limits behind the warnings

    • Heat strain: heart rate above 140 bpm for 2 min, or core 0.6 °C over the value before the walk: ease the pace. Both together: stop, rest 5 min, cooling to max (NASA-STD-3001 V2 11011).
    • Stay time: the shortest resource minus the walk back and a 30-minute reserve. A task that does not fit is dropped.
    • Suit CO₂: a steady climb without effort is treated as a scrubber fault and ends the walk. Limit 20 mmHg for at most 1 h (V2 11040).
    • Radiation: caution at 1.2 times the usual rate; a confirmed storm ends the walk, indoors within 30 min (our rule).
    • The rules decide. The assistant only explains them.

    Where the numbers come from

    • SIM stands for a suit or body sensor: a model with sourced numbers.
    • MODEL an estimate, no direct sensor exists: stamina, lactate, balance, faint risk, stay time.
    • REPLAY a real measurement replayed: the dose rate follows Curiosity's instrument at Gale crater; the storm is its record of 10 to 13 September 2017, in real time.
    • Heart rate and lactate follow shapes learned from open exercise-test datasets. Simulated data, not a medical device.
