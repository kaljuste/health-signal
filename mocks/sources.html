The data we used

Nobody has flown to Mars, so no data from such a mission exists. We looked widely, kept only what could teach a simulation something, and built one complete mission from it. This page shows the data we chose: what it is, why we chose it, what we took from it, and where you see it in the apps.

The long list of everything we looked at is our research notebook. It is not part of the product and not shown here. All data on this page is recorded history. None of it is a live feed.

What real data cannot tell us

  • Nobody has lived at Mars gravity.Every body value on Mars is a model between two known ends: weightlessness and Earth.
  • Open astronaut body data is four people for three days.The Inspiration4 crew. Long-mission changes come from published group results in NASA reports and papers.
  • Radiation depends on the ship.How much a storm reaches the crew depends on shielding. Our shielding factors are stated assumptions, not a calculation.
  • Studies on Earth are not astronauts.The exercise, sleep and heart datasets come from athletes, students and volunteers at Earth gravity.
