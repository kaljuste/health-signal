How it works

One page for the whole idea and the whole system: what each part is, what it does, and what it never does.

Everything works around one goal

Bring the astronauts back home alive, in as good health as possible.

The idea, as blocks

On Mars nothing can wait for Earth. So measuring and warning happen on board, at once. Earth still gives the deeper advice, a day later. An assistant on board puts it all into plain words.

3Earth thinks it through

Doctors, trainers and specialists look at weeks, not moments, with the time and the computers a crew lacks.

How it is built

Nobody has flown to Mars, so the mission in this preview is simulated. It is built from real data, kept in one file, and read by everything you see.

Real data

20 open datasets: radiation, Mars weather, cabin air, the human body. Recorded history, not live.

What is real today, and what comes next

Three things work today. Each one can be improved on its own, without rebuilding the other two.

1The data is really there

A whole simulated mission: 999 days, four astronauts, four Mars walks second by second. About 1.9 million values, built from real measurements, published results and stated assumptions.

Next: more real measurements, sharper models.Count it in the database
2The apps really run

The Habitat App and the Arm Unit read only that database. For real sensors, a connector fills the same tables and the same rules check them; the apps stay as they are. The connector is not built yet.

Next: real sensors, flight hardware, a whole crew.Habitat App · Arm Unit
3The advisor is live

Every answer is written at that moment by an AI model, from four things it is given: the health values, the limits and plans, Earth's instruction and the knowledge notes. The rules decide; it explains.

Next: stronger AI models, a larger knowledge base.What the assistant is given

Space projects often start this way: the whole chain runs on simulated data first, then each part is replaced or improved on its own.

Simulated data throughout. On a real mission the rules, the assistant and the data would run on the habitat computer; in this preview the AI model and the voice are cloud services reached through the server. Not a medical device.
